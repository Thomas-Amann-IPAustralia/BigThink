**Society**

[Monash University released its annual Youth Barometer, based on a survey of 527 people](https://newsreel.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2025-Australian-Youth-Barometer.pdf)
and in-depth interviews with 30 Australians aged between 18 and 24.

[The Maldives barred people born after 2006 from smoking](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2025/11/04/maldives-smoking-ban/) or purchasing tobacco
products in the country, making the archipelago the first country to implement such a
law.

[A new report Social Connections and Loneliness in OECD Countries](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/social-connections-and-loneliness-in-oecd-countries_6df2d6a0-en.html?adestraproject=OECD%20Civil%20Society%20Newsletter&utm_campaign=OECD%20Civil%20Society%20Newsletter%20-%20October%202025&utm_content=social-connections-and-loneliness-in-oecd-countries&utm_term=com&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Adestra) says new vulnerable
groups are emerging - men and young people. An international team of more than 100
[experts undertook the first systematic effort to develop evidence-based guidelines for](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.healthpol.2025.105452)
<u>[social connection.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.healthpol.2025.105452)</u>

[A study](https://academic.oup.com/jcmc/article/30/6/zmaf018/8277896?login=false) (social media accounts) found the more people see toxic behaviour from those
on their own side, the more likely they are to mirror it. Reactions to toxicity from the
opposing side were fewer. People are motivated less by outrage at the 'other side,' and
more by a desire to fit in with their own.

[Between 2008 and 2010, the average number of close social contacts increased from](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2517530122)
<u>[about two to four or five, coinciding with a marked rise in societal polarization. The](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2517530122)</u>
connection between these two developments could provide a fundamental explanation
for why societies around the world are increasingly fragmenting into ideological
bubbles.
